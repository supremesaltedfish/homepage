A BRIEF INTRODUCTION

About me

✦ ───────── ❧ ───────── ✦

Hello, I'm Nana. Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

I'm an introvert, an enthusiastic reader, and a person who can spend an unreasonable amount of time thinking about fictional people and the choices they make. I like long conversations, thoughtful analysis, good prose, and finding the small details that make a story feel alive.

Outside fandom, I'm usually reading, writing, journaling, learning something new, baking, or looking for an excuse to go for a walk in the woods.

Things I tend to love

  • Character-driven stories and messy moral questions
  • Literature, history, psychology, philosophy, and language
  • Atmospheric aesthetics, old books, moonlight, and symbolic little details
  • Kind people who can disagree thoughtfully and still have fun
  • Slow, meaningful friendships and conversations that wander

A small disclaimer

This site is a work in progress, just like me. Opinions are my own; interests change; and everything here is built for fun, not as a definitive guide to any fandom.

Glad you're here. ✧
